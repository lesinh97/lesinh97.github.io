Signatory Vintage is an independent bottler founded in Edinburgh in 1988. It buys casks from other distilleries, bottles them with little intervention, and has owned Edradour since 2002. When a distillery will not let its name go on the label, Signatory uses a letter instead. The M is widely taken to mean Macallan, though Signatory has never confirmed it.

The Cigar Reserve is exactly what the name says: sixteen years in oloroso sherry casks, bottled at 57.1 percent, and picked for the weight and the tobacco note that stand up next to smoke. It is the darker of the pair, where the 18 is the more balanced one.
