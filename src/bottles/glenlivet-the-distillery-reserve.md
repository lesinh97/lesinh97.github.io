The Glenlivet took the first licence in its glen in 1824, under George Smith. Its light, fruity Speyside style became the template much of the region followed afterwards.

Founder's Reserve arrived in 2015 as the entry level core bottling, replacing the 12 in several markets and carrying no age statement. It is matured in traditional oak alongside a proportion of first fill American oak, which is where the vanilla and the creaminess come from, and bottled at 40 percent. It is the house character with nothing in the way, which is both the point and the problem. At 40 percent there is not enough body to carry the fruit very far.
